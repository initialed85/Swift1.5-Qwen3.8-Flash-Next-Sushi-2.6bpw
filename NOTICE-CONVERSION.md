## Calibration

`sushi convert exl3` ran with **no `--calibration`**: sushi built in its standard 64 k prose+code
mix, recorded in `config.json` as

```
calib_sha256 = 7f2a5b1413545d9daf73ef13f06769914c68693121cd86f1e2611bf8d5c419c3
calib_tokens = 65536
```

The official `beamster/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Sushi-2.6bpw` pack records
`calibration=imatrix-diagonal` and its own `imatrix_sha256` instead. Encoder, codebook, window,
rate, quantizer and output-scale method match; **the calibration source does not**. Expect the
same rate and encoder, not bit-identical quality to the released tier.
